# Playtoon — business model and prices per platform

| Game | Android (Google Play) | iOS (App Store, later) | PC (Steam) | Website (GitHub Pages) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Block Quarry** | Free, **ads** (optional reward videos + capped full-screen ad between games) + optional "Remove ads" 2,99 € | Same as Android | **Paid US$ 2.99 / 2,99 €**, no ads | Free, no ads |
| **Nova Foundry** | Free, **ads** (optional reward videos only) + optional "Eternal Engine" 3,99 € | Same as Android | **Paid US$ 3.99 / 3,99 €**, no ads | Free, no ads |
| **Synth Horde** | **Paid app 2,99 €**, no ads, no purchases | Paid app 2,99 € | **Paid US$ 4.99 / 4,99 €** | Free, no ads — **see the decision below** |

Prices are suggestions; each store converts them to local prices (accept the stores' recommended regional prices).

## Why these prices
- Small premium puzzle / idle games on Steam sell mostly between US$ 1.99 and 4.99; Synth Horde has the most content
  (3 worlds, 9 weapons with evolutions, 6 characters, Heat levels), hence the top of the range.
- Synth Horde on mobile at 2,99 €: premium mobile survivors usually sit between 1,99 € and 4,99 €; a lower mobile price is normal.
- The optional purchases in the free mobile games stay at the usual "remove ads / premium boost" level (2,99–3,99 €).

## What the stores keep
| Store | Share kept by the store | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Google Play | **15 %** of your first US$ 1 M per year (paid apps and in-app products) | Enrol in the 15 % tier (automatic for new accounts) |
| Apple App Store | **15 %** with the *App Store Small Business Program* (apply once), else 30 % | Apple Developer Program: US$ 99 per year |
| Steam | **30 %** (25 % above US$ 10 M) | US$ 100 Steam Direct fee per game, paid back after US$ 1,000 of revenue |
| AdMob (ads) | Google keeps its share; you receive the ad revenue shown in AdMob | Payment once your balance reaches the threshold (≈ 70 €) |

Taxes: the stores collect and pay VAT for sales to consumers. Your own income from the stores is taxable in France
(declare it, e.g. as micro-entrepreneur — check with an accountant or your tax office).

## Decision to make: the free web versions
The three games are also free on https://flocautank.github.io/playtoon/ (no ads). This competes with the paid versions,
above all for **Synth Horde** (paid everywhere else) and for the Steam versions. Options:
1. **Keep everything free on the web** as a showcase (simplest; some buyers will notice the free version).
2. **Web = demo** (recommended for Synth Horde): first world only on the web, with a "Get the full game" link to the stores.
3. **Remove Synth Horde from the website** once it is on sale.
Nothing has been changed on the website yet: this is your call.
