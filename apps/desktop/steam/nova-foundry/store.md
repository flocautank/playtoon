## About this game
### EN
```
The star is waiting. Tap it, collect stardust, and build a foundry that never sleeps. Every screen is drawn like an old star atlas: engraved suns, gilt astrolabes, constellations traced in ink.

A STAR THAT GROWS WITH YOU
• Your star evolves through 10 stages: red, orange and yellow, white, blue giant and supergiant, hypergiant, pulsar, magnetar, quasar
• 12 cosmic forges, from a humble Spark to the Star Anvil
• 138 upgrades, forge synergies and doubling milestones
• Golden comets, dark comets, shooting stars and five sky events: eclipse, shooting-star shower, conjunction, solar flare, aurora
• Offline progress: your foundry keeps producing while the game is closed — and welcomes you back with the haul

LAYERS OF PROGRESSION
• Supernova — reset your run for Novae, a permanent production bonus
• Constellation — a permanent tree of 66 nodes, then the endless Stellar Mastery
• Asterisms — chart eight figures star by star (the Crucible, the Lyre, the Sextant, the Scales…), each strengthening one mechanic; they survive everything
• Challenges — six runs under a constraint, each with a permanent reward
• Big Bang — the second prestige: trade your Novae for Singularities, automate your Galaxy and power the Stellar Engine

ALWAYS SOMETHING TO DO
• Forge Orders — three goals cut to your current run, each paying Novae and a seal
• Guild rank — seals raise you from Apprentice to Grand master, +3% production per rank, forever
• Daily Watch — three new goals every day, a chest and a streak bonus
• Logbook — your notable Supernovae, stages, records and ranks, with the time they took
• 102 achievements, each a permanent production bonus

PLAYS YOUR WAY
• Click as much as you like, or let the Automaton click for you
• Scientific notation option for the very big numbers
• No ads, no microtransactions, no pay-to-skip. Buy once, play forever.
• Six languages: English, Français, Deutsch, Español, Italiano, Português.
```
