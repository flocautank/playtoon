Synth Horde is a third-person horde survivor drenched in synthwave. Hundreds of neon enemies close in; your weapons fire on their own. Your job: move, survive, level up and choose.

BUILD A DIFFERENT RUN EVERY TIME

• Procedural synthwave soundtrack that rises with the danger
• No microtransactions. Buy once, play forever.
