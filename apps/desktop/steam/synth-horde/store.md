## About this game
### EN
```
The grid is alive — and it wants you gone.

Synth Horde is a third-person horde survivor where the music fights back. Speakers waddle, cassettes march, TVs crackle and record needles charge — hundreds of living audio machines close in while the whole arena pulses to the beat. Your weapons fire on their own. Your job: move, survive, level up and choose.

A 3D SURVIVOR THAT MOVES
• Run, double-jump and slide across a living terrain of ramps and floating platforms
• Three worlds — the Grid, the Furnace and the low-gravity Void — each ending with a boss that attacks on the beat: a giant Boombox, a molten three-headed Amp, a floating Organ-Synth
• Readable chaos: your silhouette always shows through the crowd, enemy fire glows hot

BUILD A DIFFERENT RUN EVERY TIME
• 15 weapons, from the Blaster to homing Rockets — each evolves at level 8 with the right tome
• 12 tomes and 30 items that stack into wild builds
• Shrines, a merchant, a duplicator, elite challenges and flight-case chests to push your luck

A CAST THAT MAKES NOISE
• 9 heroes, each with their own head, colours, starting weapon and bonus — Glitch's face is a live oscilloscope
• 10 enemy families that warn you before they strike: the Needle winds up, the Tube glows, the Metronome speeds up
• Arenas of speaker columns and stage cases, ringed by speaker towers thumping to the soundtrack

PERMANENT PROGRESSION
• Earn credits every run and spend them on 9 permanent upgrades
• 60 quests that unlock new heroes, weapons and content
• Beat the game, then climb five levels of Heat — tougher hordes, bigger rewards

• Keyboard and mouse or gamepad
• Procedural synthwave soundtrack that rises with the danger
• No microtransactions. Buy once, play forever.
• Six languages: English, Français, Deutsch, Español, Italiano, Português.
```
